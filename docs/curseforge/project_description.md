<h1 align="center">⚔️ Dinamyc Combat</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Better Combat-style weapon animations for NeoForge — 33 weapon presets with first-person support.</strong></p>

<br>

---

<br>

<h2>✨ Overview</h2>

<p>Dinamyc Combat overhauls Minecraft's combat system with hand-crafted weapon animations. Swing, stab, slam, and spin your way through enemies with 33 unique weapon presets, each with its own attack pattern. Full first-person animation support and dual-wielding included.</p>

<br>

<h2>🎯 Features</h2>

<h3>🗡️ 33 Weapon Presets</h3>
<p>Each preset defines a unique attack pattern with custom animations:<br>
<strong>One-handed</strong> — Slash horizontal (left/right), Slash switch blade (left/right), Stab, Stab mounted, Swipe horizontal, Uppercut, Slam, Slam instant, Punch<br>
<strong>Two-handed</strong> — Slash horizontal (left/right), Slash vertical (left/right), Stab (left/right), Slam, Slam heavy, Spin<br>
<strong>Dual-handed</strong> — Slash cross, Slash uncross, Stab<br>
<strong>Poses</strong> — One-handed backwards, Two-handed bow/crossbow/heavy/katana/polearm/scythe/sword</p>

<h3>🎮 Hold-to-Attack</h3>
<p>Hold click for continuous attacks with alternating hands for dual wielding. Each weapon preset defines its own attack rhythm.</p>

<h3>🖐️ Off-Hand Support</h3>
<p>Left-handed animations via <code>MirrorIfLeftHandModifier</code> — weapons in the off-hand automatically mirror their animations.</p>

<h3>👁️ First-Person Animation</h3>
<p>Full player model renders in first person (THIRD_PERSON_MODEL), showing both arms, armor, and weapons with the same animations as third person.</p>

<h3>🛡️ Armor Always Visible</h3>
<p>Armor renders correctly in all perspectives — no more invisible armor during attacks.</p>

<h3>⚙️ Configurable</h3>
<p>Full client-side configuration screen (accessible via Mods → Dinamyc Combat → Config):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>First-person arms</strong> — toggle arm visibility during attacks (<code>isShowingArmsInFirstPerson</code>)</li>
<li><strong>Second hand</strong> — show/hide the off-hand or two-handed weapon in first person (<code>isShowingOtherHandFirstPerson</code>)</li>
<li><strong>Hold-to-attack</strong> — hold click for continuous attacks, or tap for single strikes</li>
<li><strong>Mining with weapons</strong> — allow/prevent mining while holding a weapon</li>
<li><strong>Swing through grass</strong> — attack through plants when targeting an enemy</li>
<li><strong>Sweep arc effect</strong> — toggle the vanilla-style sweep arc visual during attacks (<code>isShowingSweepEffect</code>)</li>
<li><strong>Weapon swing sounds</strong> — adjust volume or disable</li>
<li><strong>Crosshair highlight</strong> — visual indicator when targeting enemies</li>
<li><strong>Attack range tooltip</strong> — show weapon reach in item tooltips</li>
<li><strong>Axe as weapon</strong> — treat axes as combat weapons with full animation support</li>
</ul>

<h2>🎮 How to Use</h2>

<ol>
<li>Install the mod. Player Animation Core is bundled inside the jar.</li>
<li>Equip any sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, or hoe and attack enemies.</li>
<li>Hold the attack button for continuous combos.</li>
<li>Dual-wield two weapons to use both hands.</li>
</ol>

<br>

<h2>🔌 Compatibility</h2>

<ul>
<li>Compatible with Vampires &amp; Vampires — no mixin conflicts</li>
<li>Works with all vanilla and modded weapons registered as swords, axes, pickaxes, shovels, or hoes</li>
<li>Fully client-side — no server-side installation required</li>
</ul>
